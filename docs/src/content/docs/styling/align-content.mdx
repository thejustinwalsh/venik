Align content defines the distribution of lines along the cross axis. It only has an effect when items wrap onto several lines with [flex wrap](../flex-wrap/).

**Stretch (default)**: Stretch wrapped lines to fill the container's cross axis.

**Flex start**: Pack wrapped lines at the start of the container's cross axis.

**Flex end**: Pack wrapped lines at the end of the container's cross axis.

**Center**: Pack wrapped lines in the center of the container's cross axis.

**Space between**: Space wrapped lines evenly across the cross axis, distributing remaining space between the lines.

**Space around**: Space wrapped lines evenly, distributing remaining space around the lines, so the first line gets half a gap before it and the last line half a gap after it.

**Space evenly**: Space wrapped lines evenly, with the gaps between lines and between the lines and the container edges all equal.

```ts
node.setAlignContent(Align.SpaceBetween);
```
