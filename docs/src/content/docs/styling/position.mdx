**Static (default)**: The node is laid out in the normal flow of its flex container. It ignores [insets](../insets/) and does not form a [containing block](../../advanced/containing-block/) for absolutely positioned descendants. Yoga defaults to `relative`; baba-yaga follows CSS.

**Relative**: Like static, the node takes part in the flexbox algorithm and takes up space in its container. [Insets](../insets/) then offset it from its normal position. A relative node always forms a [containing block](../../advanced/containing-block/).

**Absolute**: The node is taken out of the flow of its flex container. It takes up no space and does not affect the position of its siblings. [Insets](../insets/) place it relative to its [containing block](../../advanced/containing-block/).

```ts
node.setPositionType(PositionType.Absolute);
```
