Align items describes how children are aligned along the cross axis of their container. It is the cross-axis counterpart of [justify content](../justify-content/).

**Stretch (default)**: Stretch children to fill the container's cross axis.

**Flex start**: Align children to the start of the container's cross axis.

**Flex end**: Align children to the end of the container's cross axis.

**Center**: Center children along the container's cross axis.

**Baseline**: Align children along a common baseline. A child can be made the reference baseline for its parent with `setIsReferenceBaseline(true)`, and a leaf can supply its own baseline with `setBaselineFunc`.

```ts
container.setAlignItems(Align.Center);
```

## Align self

Align self has the same options and effect as align items, but applies to a single child, overriding what its parent set with align items.

```ts
child.setAlignSelf(Align.FlexEnd);
```
