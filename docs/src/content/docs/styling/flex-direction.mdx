Flex direction controls the direction in which the children of a node are laid out, also called the main axis. The cross axis is perpendicular to it and is the axis along which [wrapped](../flex-wrap/) lines stack.

**Row (default)**: Lay children out from left to right. With wrapping, the next line starts under the first item.

**Row reverse**: Lay children out from right to left. With wrapping, the next line starts under the first item on the right.

**Column**: Lay children out from top to bottom. With wrapping, the next line starts to the right of the first item.

**Column reverse**: Lay children out from bottom to top. With wrapping, the next line starts to the right of the first item at the bottom.

Yoga defaults to `column`; baba-yaga follows CSS and defaults to `row`.

```ts
node.setFlexDirection(FlexDirection.Column);
```
